On Friday, the art group visited the hilltop shelter to study a night sky journal. It was a fresh
morning, and the group felt cheerful. the art group set a star chart beside the south window before
the first task began. A steady note explained how the group would prepare a useful display. When
the wind grew stronger, everyone paused and checked the small compass again. They used the
small compass to record the position of each bright star and compare each result. The group
noticed a missing label, so they adjusted the plan and continued. After lunch, the art group asked
whether the new plan could prepare a useful display. The answer was clear: the moon appeared
above the quiet ridge. By evening, the group had finished a clear page of observations, and the
next step seemed possible.
