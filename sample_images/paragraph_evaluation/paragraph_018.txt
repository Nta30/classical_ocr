On Saturday, Jamal's neighbors visited the computer lab to study a small
robotics project. It was a bright morning, and the group felt positive. Jamal's
neighbors set a plastic wheel beside the center mat before the first task
began. A clear note explained how the group would explain the result. When
the wind grew stronger, everyone paused and checked the spare battery
again. They used the spare battery to record the robot path and compare
each result. The group noticed a heavy box, so they adjusted the plan and
continued. After lunch, Jamal's neighbors asked whether the new plan could
explain the result. The answer was clear: the robot turned faster after its
wheels were aligned. By evening, the group had finished a smooth final run,
and the next step seemed possible.
