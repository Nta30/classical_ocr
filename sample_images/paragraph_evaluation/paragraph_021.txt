On Tuesday, Maya and Luis visited the library archive to study an old city map. It was a cool
morning, and the group felt calm. Maya and Luis set a folded map beside the east gate before
the first task began. A clear note explained how the group would check every detail. When the
lights flickered, everyone paused and checked the desk lamp again. They used the desk lamp to
record route marks and compare each result. The group noticed a torn corner, so they adjusted
the plan and continued. After lunch, Maya and Luis asked whether the new plan could check
every detail. The answer was clear: the river bridge was missing from the oldest copy. By
evening, the group had finished a clean map for the town walk, and the next step seemed
possible.
