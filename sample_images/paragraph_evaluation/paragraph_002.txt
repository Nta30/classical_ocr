garden. It was a warm morning, and the group felt steady. Nora's class set a
clay pot beside the seed beds before the first task began. A simple note
explained how the group would finish the first draft. When the wind grew
stronger, everyone paused and checked the rain gauge again. They used the
rain gauge to record daily growth and compare each result. The group
noticed a wet patch, so they adjusted the plan and continued. After lunch,
Nora's class asked whether the new plan could finish the first draft. The
answer was clear: the first sprouts appeared after five days. By evening, the
