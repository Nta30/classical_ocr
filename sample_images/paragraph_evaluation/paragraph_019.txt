On Sunday, two patient builders visited the village square to study a market day plan. It
was a windy morning, and the group felt curious. two patient builders set a price list
beside the front counter before the first task began. A simple note explained how the
group would set up the fruit stand. When the lights flickered, everyone paused and
checked the pocket scale again. They used the pocket scale to record the weight of
each basket and compare each result. The group noticed a missing sign, so they
adjusted the plan and continued. After lunch, two patient builders asked whether the
new plan could complete the careful test. The answer was clear: fresh apples sold best
before the rain. By evening, the group had finished a busy and friendly market, and the
next step seemed possible.
