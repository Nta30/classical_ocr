On Friday, Eli and his sister visited the gym entrance to study a paper
airplane test. It was a clear morning, and the group felt careful. Eli and his
sister set a yellow plane beside the center line before the first task began. A
useful note explained how the group would make the next step easier. When
the wind grew stronger, everyone paused and checked the tape measure
again. They used the tape measure to record the distance of every flight and
compare each result. The group noticed a narrow path, so they adjusted the
plan and continued. After lunch, Eli and his sister asked whether the new
plan could make the next step easier. The answer was clear: the widest
wings traveled farthest. By evening, the group had finished a fair test for the
next lesson, and the next step seemed possible.
