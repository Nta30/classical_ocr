On Friday, the young crew visited the school hall to study a small music room. It was a
cloudy morning, and the group felt focused. the young crew set a blue notebook beside
the piano bench before the first task began. A fresh note explained how the group
would share the work with others. When the lights flickered, everyone paused and
checked the tuning fork again. They used the tuning fork to record the sound of each
string and compare each result. The group noticed a loose music sheet, so they
adjusted the plan and continued. After lunch, the young crew asked whether the new
plan could share the work with others. The answer was clear: the quiet notes carried
farther than expected. By evening, the group had finished a simple evening
performance, and the next step seemed possible.
