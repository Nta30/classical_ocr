On Tuesday, the science club visited the station window to study a weather journal. It
was a still morning, and the group felt hopeful. the science club set a pocket calendar
beside the south wall before the first task began. A bright note explained how the
group would track the clouds. When the lights flickered, everyone paused and checked
the glass thermometer again. They used the glass thermometer to record the morning
temperature and compare each result. The group noticed a sudden shower, so they
adjusted the plan and continued. After lunch, the science club asked whether the new
plan could save time for review. The answer was clear: dark clouds arrived before the
wind changed. By evening, the group had finished a full week of weather notes, and
the next step seemed possible.
