On Thursday, Maya and Luis visited the east classroom to study a school science fair.
It was a cool morning, and the group felt calm. Maya and Luis set a project card beside
the tall window before the first task began. A short note explained how the group would
check every detail. When the lights flickered, everyone paused and checked the glass
jar again. They used the glass jar to record the time each seed needed and compare
each result. The group noticed a cracked lid, so they adjusted the plan and continued.
After lunch, Maya and Luis asked whether the new plan could check every detail. The
answer was clear: warm light helped the beans grow faster. By evening, the group had
finished a neat display for the judges, and the next step seemed possible.
