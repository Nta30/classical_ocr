On Monday, four curious students visited the design lab to study a bridge building
challenge. It was a misty morning, and the group felt ready. four curious students set a
wooden block beside the test table before the first task began. A short note explained
how the group would support the longest span. When the lights flickered, everyone
paused and checked the hanging weight again. They used the hanging weight to
record the strength of each joint and compare each result. The group noticed a weak
corner, so they adjusted the plan and continued. After lunch, four curious students
asked whether the new plan could solve the small problem. The answer was clear: a
wide base held more weight than a tall frame. By evening, the group had finished a
sturdy bridge design, and the next step seemed possible.
